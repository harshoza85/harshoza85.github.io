Research Interests

Isotope Hydrometeorology

Analyzing stable isotope (δ18O, Δ17O, δ2H) systematics in daily and event-based precipitation to identify moisture sources, sub-cloud evaporation, convective regimes, and orographic influences across Asia.

Coastal Hydrogeology

Quantifying submarine groundwater discharge (SGD), salinity sources, and surface water–groundwater interactions along coastal aquifer systems using environmental isotopes and hydrochemical tracers.

Monsoon & Climate Dynamics

Linking multidecadal precipitation isotope trends with atmospheric reanalysis fields to study summer monsoon rainfall variability, trend reversals, and large-scale ocean-atmosphere reorganization.

Vapor Phase & Kinetic Fractionation

Studying ground-level water vapor dynamics, kinetic fractionation during supersaturated condensation, and recycled moisture contributions across tropical and high-altitude mountain regimes.
