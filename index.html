Postdoctoral Fellow

Harsh Oza, Ph.D.

Department of Geosciences · National Taiwan University

Earth Science researcher with 10 years of experience in isotope hydrology and climate dynamics. My research combines analytical measurement and interpretation of water stable isotopes (δ18O, Δ17O, δ2H) to investigate hydrometeorological processes, continental moisture transport, Asian monsoon variability, and coastal groundwater dynamics.
