Research Focus

Isotope Hydrology & Meteorology

Analyzing spatial and temporal stable isotope (δ18O, Δ17O, δ2H) variations in precipitation to decipher moisture sources, raindrop evaporation, convective regimes, and orographic effects.

Coastal Hydrogeology & Salinity

Quantifying coastal groundwater-surface water interactions, submarine groundwater discharge (SGD), and recharge dynamics in aquifer systems using hydrochemistry and isotopic tracing.

Climate Dynamics & Large Datasets

Connecting meteorological reanalysis datasets with multidecadal precipitation isotope trends to evaluate Indian summer monsoon shifts, tropical warming, and atmospheric circulation extremes.

Analytical Laser Spectroscopy

Operating and calibrating Cavity Ring-Down Spectroscopy (CRDS) and Isotope Ratio Mass Spectrometry (IRMS) systems for high-precision water vapor, liquid water, and noble gas tracers.
